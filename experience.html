Executed audit procedures through verification, vouching, tracing, reconciliation, and observation to examine financial transactions, validate supporting evidence, assess accounting records, and identify discrepancies.

Audit Procedures & Working Approach

1. Document Verification
  • Examined financial statements, general ledgers, trial balances, bank statements, tax records, invoices, receipts, and other supporting documents.
  • Verified the consistency and accuracy of information across financial and accounting records.
  • Compared recorded transactions with supporting documentation to identify missing, inconsistent, or unclear information.
  • Reviewed financial statement disclosures against underlying accounting records.
2. Vouching & Tracing
  • Performed vouching by tracing recorded transactions to their original supporting documents.
  • Performed tracing by following source documents into accounting records to examine the completeness of transaction recording.
  • Checked transaction details, amounts, dates, and supporting evidence.
  • Identified discrepancies and followed up on transactions requiring further verification.
3. Reconciliation & Financial Data Review
  • Performed bank reconciliations by comparing bank statements with accounting records to identify differences, outstanding transactions, and unusual items.
  • Performed tax reconciliations by comparing tax records and supporting documents with related accounting records to identify discrepancies.
  • Compared trial balances with general ledger balances and related financial information.
  • Reviewed financial statement disclosures against underlying accounting records.
  • Compared third party confirmations with client records and investigated identified differences.
  • Followed up on reconciliation discrepancies and outstanding supporting information.
4. Observation & Audit Assessment
  • Observed relevant business activities, operational processes, or conditions as required by assigned audit procedures.
  • Considered business and industry characteristics when examining transactions and assessing potential risk areas.
  • Documented relevant observations and information supporting audit procedures.
5. Audit Documentation & Follow-up
  • Collected and evaluated audit evidence to support testing and verification results.
  • Prepared and maintained audit working papers using ATLAS (Audit Tool and Linked Archive System).
  • Documented procedures performed, findings, supporting evidence, and follow-up items.
  • Followed up on missing documents, unclear transactions, and identified discrepancies with relevant client representatives.
