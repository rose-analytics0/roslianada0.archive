Data Insight Journey

From Raw Data to Meaningful Information

Data Insight Journey is a personal learning and knowledge-sharing series documenting my exploration of data processing, spreadsheet techniques, accounting information, and practical problem-solving.

Through this series, I aim to share experiences from working with raw and unstructured information, exploring spreadsheet formulas, identifying data inconsistencies, and developing structured solutions using Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

The series also connects my experiences in production operations, inventory control, accounting, external auditing, and data analytics, showing how different types of information can be organized, checked, and transformed into something more useful.

Rather than simply presenting final results, I want to explore the process behind the work: understanding the data, checking its accuracy, identifying discrepancies, applying appropriate methods, and turning information into something more structured and reliable.
